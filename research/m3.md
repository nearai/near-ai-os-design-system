## 1. M3 Expressive: what it is, research basis, principles, what changed

### 1.1 Definition and launch
- Launched 13–14 May 2025 (The Android Show: I/O Edition, then Google I/O 2025), alongside Android 16 on Pixel. [blog: building-with-m3-expressive, dated 13 May 2025]
- "M3 Expressive isn't a new version of the system. We're not deprecating M3, and this isn't 'M4.'" It is "a set of new features, updated components, and design tactics for creating emotionally impactful UX." [blog]
- Compose describes Expressive APIs as "an expansion pack to M3; you can opt in". [blog: material-is-compose-first, 19 May 2026]

### 1.2 Research basis [dg][blog]
- 46 studies, 18,000+ participants worldwide, hundreds of designs, run over ~3 years (2022–2025). Methods were eye tracking, surveys and focus groups, preference experiments, and usability tests.
- Expressive designs are preferred across all ages; the preference reaches 87% among 18–24-year-olds.
- They rated higher on "energetic, emotive, positive vibe, creative, playful, friendly". Desirability rose: +32% subculture perception, +34% modernity, +30% rebelliousness.
- Usability: across 10 apps, participants spotted key UI elements up to 4× faster in Expressive versions. Example: in an email app, the Send button was made larger, placed just above the keyboard and given the secondary colour. Age effects in fixation time disappeared, so 45+ users performed like younger users.
- The research also lists limits: "Context still matters".
  - Breaking conventions hurt usability. Album art "helter-skelter" in place of a list scored badly, and so did removing text labels from email actions.
  - A strong minority preferred calmer versions.
  - Banking-type contexts need restraint.
- Earlier attribute framework (2024): hierarchy, utility and style questions. In an M2-vs-M3 email test, M3 won on hierarchy (81% / 92%) and utility (77% / 76%); M2 was rated more playful (65%). This fed the Expressive brief. [blog: testing-material-3]
- Component-level studies looked at:
  - which progress indicators make waiting feel faster (leading to the wavy indicators);
  - how big a button can be;
  - floating-toolbar perception;
  - "in many cases, we chose to exceed existing standards for tap target size, color contrast". [dg]

### 1.3 What shipped in May 2025 [blog][m3]
- **14 new or updated components:**
  - New: button groups, FAB menu, loading indicator, split button, toolbars (docked and floating).
  - Updated: app bars, common buttons, extended FAB, FABs, icon buttons, navigation bar, navigation rail, progress indicators, sliders.
- **Motion physics system:** spring tokens, split into spatial vs effects × fast/default/slow, in expressive and standard schemes. It replaces easing + duration.
- **Emphasized typography:** 15 emphasized styles on top of the 15 baseline styles; variable-font support.
- **Shape:** a 35-shape library, built-in shape morph, new corner steps (large-increased 20dp, extra-large-increased 32dp, extra-extra-large 48dp), and "full" redefined as truly circular instead of 50% of size.
- **Colour:**
  - three contrast levels: standard, medium (≥3:1) and high (7:1), all tokenised;
  - "vibrant" schemes for sharper hierarchy;
  - the on-container roles were already made more colourful in Aug 2024.

### 1.4 Expressive tactics (the "how") [blog][m3 usability]
1. **Use a variety of shapes.** Mix round and square for tension; break from the surrounding shape style to draw attention.
2. **Apply rich and nuanced colours.** Contrast primary, secondary and tertiary roles and surface tones for hierarchy.
3. **Guide attention with typography.** Use emphasized styles, heavier weights, larger sizes and "editorial" moments.
4. **Contain content for emphasis.** Group content into containers; give the key task "ample space and the brightest surface mapping".
5. **Add fluid and natural motion.** Use shape morph, surface effects and springs.
6. **Leverage component flexibility.** Adapt components to context, foldables and large screens.
7. **Combine tactics to create hero moments.** A hero moment is "brief, delightful, surprising, and unexpected", limited to "one or two" per product.
   - To find one, ask: is the interaction emotionally impactful, and is it a key interaction?
   - The Aura sample app shows the method:
     - **Primary goal:** an extra-large button, placed low and reachable, in the primary colour.
     - **Hero moment:** the breathing flower, built from the Flower and Sunny shapes on springs.
     - **Secondary and tertiary screens** deliberately use fewer tactics.
     - **Version 1 → 2:** settings grouped into list items, and one XL CTA.
   - Rule: "Not using too many expressive tactics at the same time as they can be distracting." [m3 foundations/usability]

### 1.6 Deprecations and "no longer recommended" (system level)
Component-level deprecations are in §6.
- Easing and duration motion: "no longer maintained". Products should migrate to springs. It is still used for full-screen **transitions**, which "will eventually be updated". [m3 motion]
- Surface tint and elevation-tinted surfaces: deprecated in favour of tone-based surface-container roles (since Feb 2023). [m3 elevation][blog]
- "Window size classes" is now "breakpoints". The 3-class model was extended to 5 classes (large and extra-large added in 2025). [m3 layout][ad]
- Baseline token sets such as `md.comp.button` (baseline): "no longer recommended". Use the size sets (`md.comp.button.{xsmall…xlarge}`) together with the colour sets (`md.comp.button.{filled,tonal,outlined,elevated,text}`). [tok]
- Component spacing tokens named `*-space` (`leading-space`, `between-space`) are being replaced by `padding`, `gap` and `margin` naming. [m3 spacing]
- Material Views (MDC-Android): maintenance only after 1.14.0. Material Web: no Expressive work announced. [blog][m3]

## 2. Foundations

### 2.1 Accessibility (targets, contrast, focus, structure) [m3 foundations/designing, overview, writing]
- **Touch targets:** at least **48×48dp**, which is about 9mm (7–10mm is recommended). The page notes "iOS recommends 44×44dp". **Pointer targets:** at least **44×44dp**. **Target spacing:** ≥8dp between targets.
- **Icon targets:** a 24dp icon gets a 48dp target. With a mouse and keyboard as primary input, a 20dp icon may use a 40dp target. [m3 icons]
- **State layers:** 40dp inside a 48dp target. [m3 states]
- **XR targets:** ≥56×56dp, with a ≥48dp visual affordance and a 4dp offset. [m3 xr]
- **Text contrast:** ≥4.5:1 for small text; ≥3:1 for large text (14pt bold / 18pt regular and up) and for graphics. Disabled states are exempt.
- **Non-text contrast:** clustered elements, such as button groups, need ≥3:1 container-to-background contrast. Stand-alone prominent elements, such as a FAB, need not meet it.
- **User-selectable contrast levels:** standard, medium (≥3:1 minimum), and high (7:1, applied to content but not containers). They are applied automatically to light and dark themes through colour roles. [m3 color/system]
- **Text resizing:** support ≥200%. Text and line height scale; padding and inter-element spacing stay at 1×. Where the OS does not control scale, offer 1.5× and 2× multipliers. [m3 writing]
- **Truncation:** an ellipsis is only acceptable if the full text is reachable through a tooltip or link.
- **Focus:**
  - Tab and Shift+Tab move between components; arrow keys move within them; Enter activates; Esc dismisses.
  - Define initial focus. Dialogs move focus in and return it to the trigger on close.
  - Custom shortcuts should use ≥2 keys by default; single-key shortcuts must be remappable or focus-scoped.
- **Keyboard focus indicator tokens [tok]:**

  | Token | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `md.sys.state.focus-indicator.thickness` | 3dp |
  | `outer-offset` | 2dp |
  | `inner-offset` | −3dp |

  Compose added **inset focus rings** (Apr–Jul 2026) as an alternative to the opacity-based focus layer. [cx]
- **Web structure:** use the 8 ARIA landmarks (navigation, search, main, banner, complementary, contentinfo, region, form) and sequential headings with a single H1. Labels must not repeat the role name ("button").
- **Selection on desktop:** checkboxes are always visible when selection is the primary activity. Otherwise a checkbox appears on hover, and on all items once one is selected. [m3 selection]

### 2.2 Adaptive design, breakpoints, panes, canonical layouts (updated May 2026) [m3 layout][cx][ad]

**Breakpoints (formerly window size classes)**

| Breakpoint | Width (dp) | Typical devices | Panes (recommended / allowed) | Navigation | Dialog | Supplemental selection | Margins / spacer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compact | <600 | Phone portrait | 1 | Navigation bar, or *modal* expanded nav rail | Basic or full-screen | Bottom sheet | 16dp / n.a. |
| Medium | 600–839 | Tablet portrait, unfolded foldable portrait | 1 (2 only for low-density content, 50/50 split) | Single pane: collapsed **nav rail**. Two panes: **nav bar**. Plus modal expanded rail | Basic | Menu | 24 / 24 |
| Expanded | 840–1199 | Phone landscape, tablet landscape, foldable landscape, small desktop | 2 (1 for dense media) | Collapsed or expanded rail (modal or standard) | Basic | Menu | 24 / 24 |
| Large | 1200–1599 | Desktop, large tablets | 2 (1 for dense media) | Collapsed or expanded rail | Basic | Menu | 24 / 24 |
| Extra-large | ≥1600 | Desktop, ultrawide | 2, or 1–3 (third = standard side sheet) | Expanded rail best | Basic | Menu | 24 / 24 |

- Height classes (Android): compact <480, medium 480–899, expanded ≥900dp. They are rarely used, except for tabletop or short windows. [cx WindowSizeClass][ad]
- In Compose, large and extra-large must be opted into: `currentWindowAdaptiveInfo(supportLargeAndXLargeWidth = true)`, using `BREAKPOINTS_V2`. [ad][cx]
- Pane directive (Compose adaptive) [cx PaneScaffoldDirective]:
  - Compact and medium: 1 horizontal partition. Expanded: 2. Large and above: 3. Spacer: 24dp.
  - Default preferred pane width: **360dp**, or **412dp** at large/XL.
  - Vertical 2-pane split (24dp spacer) applies in tabletop posture, or with 1 column plus expanded height. Default preferred pane height: 420dp.
- Fixed pane widths [m3]: 360dp at expanded; 412dp at large and XL. Side sheets have a max width of 400dp. Drag-handle snap points: 360dp, 412dp, and a visually centred split.
- **Split-pane rule:** the nav rail shrinks only the leading pane, so that the spacer stays visually centred at 50% of the window.
- **Reachability (medium, landscape tablets and foldables):** the top 25% of the screen is out of thumb reach; avoid essential actions there. [m3]
- **Line length:** keep 40–60 characters per line across breakpoints. [m3]
- **Five adaptation questions:** reveal, divide, resize, reposition, swap.
- **Common swaps:**
  - nav bar → collapsed rail → expanded rail;
  - full-screen dialog → basic dialog;
  - bottom sheet → menu;
  - FAB ↔ extended FAB;
  - docked pane (bottom sheet) → floating or co-planar pane on larger screens.

  "Don't swap a button for a chip."

**Layout scaffold** [m3 scaffold]
- **Bars:** the app bar (top), the navigation bar (bottom), and the safety regions (system bars).
- **Rails:** the perimeter regions around panes:
  - toolbars, chat input and FABs on compact (top and bottom rails);
  - the navigation rail (leading) and companion or vertical toolbars (trailing) on large screens.
  - In XR, rail components become **orbiters**.
- **Panes:** "all content must be in a pane". There are 1–3 panes; at least one is flexible.
  - Panes can be fixed or flexible, and permanent or temporary.
  - Display modes: **co-planar**, **floating** (a dialog-like pane above content; the scrim is optional on large screens) and **docked** (pinned to an edge, like a bottom sheet).
  - Adaptive strategies: **show/hide**, **levitate** (to floating or docked) and **reflow** (stack vertically).
  - Resizing: persistent (remember the user's width) or temporary (supporting pane; resets to default).
  - Focus order follows the visual order. Modal floating panes move focus in and return it on close.
- **Rulers:** global alignment lines for margin, bar/safety, title, content 1–4 and rail. Margin rulers can widen or narrow for expression; for example, a full-bleed photo grid with a search bar that uses wider margins.
- **Grids:** 4 columns at compact and 8 at foldable/medium in the examples. The site no longer publishes a fixed 12-column spec; columns, width and spacing "change based on breakpoints". [m3 grids]

**Canonical layouts** [m3]
- **Feed:** a grid of cards. Single column at compact; columns increase from medium upward.
- **List-detail:**

  | Breakpoint | Visible panes |
  |---|---|
  | Compact | 1 |
  | Medium | 1 (2 OK) |
  | Expanded and up | 2 |

  - The back button appears only in single-pane mode; the selected state appears only in two-pane mode.
  - Unfolding with no selection shows an empty detail placeholder.
  - Folding with a selection shows the detail view.
  - Keep scroll position and read state.
- **Supporting pane:**
  - The primary pane takes about 2/3.
  - At compact and medium, the supporting pane goes below (flexible) or into a bottom sheet.
  - At expanded, it goes leading or trailing, fixed at 360dp.
- **Custom "layering":** floating panes over content for focused tasks such as a basket, comments or event creation.

**Devices and modes** [m3 layout-overview]
- **Mobile:** phones, foldables, tablets. Window modes: full-screen, split-screen, bubbles.
- **Desktop:** free-form windows. A tablet with keyboard and mouse becomes "desktop", as does Android connected to a monitor.
- **Spatial (XR):** home space and full space. Design for touch, pointer and physical keyboard "regardless of device type".

### 2.3 Spacing system (new, May 2026) [m3 styles/spacing][tok]
- Base unit is `md.sys.measurement.space100 = 8dp`. Tokens:

  | Token | Value |
  |---|---|
  | space0 | 0 |
  | space25 | 2 |
  | space50 | 4 |
  | space75 | 6 |
  | space100 | 8 |
  | space125 | 10 |
  | space150 | 12 |
  | space175 | 14 |
  | space200 | 16 |
  | space250 | 20 |
  | space300 | 24 |
  | space400 | 32 |
  | space450 | 36 |
  | space500 | 40 |
  | space600 | 48 |
  | space700 | 56 |
  | space800 | 64 |
  | space900 | 72 |

  Extend by multiplier; for example, `space225 = 18dp`.
- **Vocabulary:** padding (inside), gap (between; horizontal, vertical, or element-named such as icon-label gap) and margin (outside). Positions are top, bottom, leading, trailing, horizontal and vertical; leading and trailing flip in RTL. "Use padding & gaps before margins."
- **Adapt mappings per form factor** (mobile, desktop, car, XR, TV) and per density setting.
  - Density scale: 0, −1, −2, −3. Each step typically removes 4dp of vertical padding.
  - Density is **opt-in only**. Never go below a 48×48 target by default. Do not densify menus, snackbars or dialogs.
- **Spacing stays at 1× when text scales to 200%.**
- Only Compose ships the spacing tokens today; Views and Web are listed as "Unavailable".

### 2.4 Interaction: states, inputs, gestures [m3][tok][cx]
- **States:** enabled, disabled, hover, focused, pressed, dragged, plus selected/activated.
  - A state layer is drawn in the **content ("on") colour**, between the container and the content.
  - Opacities: **hover 8%, focus 10%, pressed 10%, dragged 16%**.
  - Disabled: content at 38% opacity (`md.sys.state.disabled.state-layer-opacity 0.38`). Disabled containers typically use on-surface at 12% (per component tokens). Disabled elements don't get focus or hover, and FABs are never shown disabled; hide them instead.
  - Only one hover, focus, pressed or dragged state may exist in a layout at a time.
  - Navigation and containment components (app bars, nav bar/rail/drawer, dialogs, sheets) do not take whole-component hover, focus or press; only their actionable children do.
- **Pressed feedback:** a ripple. In Expressive, buttons also **morph shape** on press (see §6). On hover, elevated elements rise +1 level.
- **Inputs:**
  - Mouse: show a cursor; right-click opens a context menu; hover shows tooltips.
  - Cursors: pointer, hand for links, resize arrows, I-beam. Double-click selects a word; triple-click selects a paragraph.
  - Text selection: no touch handles with a mouse, but always show touch handles for touch input.
  - Trackpad: two-finger scroll; pinch to zoom.
  - Physical keyboard: hides the on-screen keyboard; Enter sends; Space plays or pauses; Esc dismisses.
- **Gestures:** tap, double tap (zoom), long press, scroll/pan, swipe (peer views or actions), drag, pick-up-and-move (long press + drag), pinch, and compound gestures.
  - **Predictive back** on Android works with bottom sheet, navigation bar, navigation rail, search bar and side sheet.
  - Selection mode starts with a long press or an avatar tap; long press + drag selects ranges.
- **Compose 2026 "precision pointer component sizing"** gives pointer-optimised paddings (buttons, dialogs, segmented menus). It is the code-level answer to desktop density. [cx]

### 2.5 Content design and writing [m3 content-design]
- Use sentence case; use second person ("you") and avoid mixing "I/we" with "you"; use contractions; use serial commas.
- No periods in short UI strings, no colons in headings, few exclamation marks, no ampersands in body text, no ALL-CAPS blocks.
- Explain consequences; write scannable text.
- Alt text, global writing and notifications each have dedicated pages. Navigation items must have labels.

### 2.6 Design tokens and customization [m3 design-tokens, customization]
- Token tiers:
  - `md.ref.*`: palette, typeface and other raw values; context-free.
  - `md.sys.*`: theme decisions; context-dependent for light/dark, contrast, 1P/3P, iOS, density and language height.
  - `md.comp.*`: component attributes.

  Format: `md.comp.fab.primary.container.color`. Component tokens are grouped by state, then by element.
- **The token data itself carries contexts that matter for a hybrid system** [tok]:
  - **iOS** context overrides: iOS plain typeface = **"System"** (SF), iOS "bold text" weight bumps, and iOS-specific surface values for dark **elevated** surfaces (e.g. surface-container `#282A2C`, high `#333537`, highest `#37393B`, low `#1E1F20`, lowest `#1B1B1B`); scrim `#000000` at 32% on iOS 1P.
  - 1P (Google baseline, Google Sans and Google Sans Flex) vs 3P (Roboto / Roboto Flex).
  - Language-height contexts for type.
  - Medium and high contrast.
- **Customization ladder** (shape, type, colour):
  1. change the system style value, e.g. the "medium" radius;
  2. remap a component to a different style, e.g. button shape full → medium;
  3. change the shape family from rounded to cut;
  4. add custom tokens for patterns, e.g. `surface-content.padding.horizontal`.

  Material warns that customising the type scale can block future token updates.

### 2.7 Building for all devices: XR, watches, AI glasses [m3 xr, watches][m3 whats-new]
- **XR (Android XR):**
  - Default spatial panel: **1024×720dp**, launched 1.75m away, with **32dp corners**; max 2560×1800dp; movable 0.75–5m.
  - Place the panel centre 5° below eye level; keep primary content in the central 41° of the field of view.
  - Layouts: flat, curved or arbitrary panel groups.
  - **Orbiters** host navigation rails, app bars and nav bars:
    - offset positioning is 20dp from the panel; inset positioning overlaps ≤12dp, and never more than half the orbiter's height;
    - the app bar orbiter sits at the top centre; the nav bar orbiter sits at the bottom.
  - Spatial elevation replaces shadows; a scrim can float "several centimetres" behind a dialog.
  - Use **basic dialogs only**, never full-screen ones. Dialogs use surface-container-high or highest, sit at the "highest resting level", scale uniformly and fade.
  - Use standard easing with long durations, a stable horizon, and teleport instead of continuous motion.
  - Min body text: 14dp. Use surface roles so passthrough dims automatically.
  - Interaction: hand rays plus pinch, eye-gaze hover, voice, keyboard/mouse and 6DoF controllers. Never require two hands.
- **Watches (Wear OS M3 Expressive):**
  - round screens; design from a 192dp watch, and show more content above 225dp;
  - edge-hugging containers; arc text; "build from black" colour;
  - the same spring system, with device-specific values.
- **AI glasses:** a separate system, "Jetpack Compose Glimmer", for transparent displays (I/O 2026). Not in scope, but noted.

## 3. Styles

### 3.1 Colour [m3 color/*][tok][blog tone-based-surface]
- **Pipeline:** source colour (wallpaper, in-app content, or hand-picked) → MCU algorithm → 5 key colours (primary, secondary, tertiary, neutral, neutral-variant) → tonal palettes (tones 0–100; 13 standard stops plus extras such as 4, 6, 12, 17, 22, 24, 87, 92, 94, 96) → **26 standard roles**, 45 including add-ons, for light and dark.
  - The colour space is **HCT** (hue 0–360; chroma 0 to ~120; tone 0–100 = luminance).
  - Contrast is guaranteed by tone deltas. Example: tones 50 and 98 give ≥3:1; tones 30 and 98 give ≥7:1.
- **Roles:**
  - primary, on-primary, primary-container, on-primary-container, and the same four for secondary, tertiary and error;
  - surface, on-surface, on-surface-variant;
  - surface-container-lowest, -low, (default), -high, -highest;
  - inverse-surface, inverse-on-surface, inverse-primary;
  - outline, outline-variant;
  - scrim, shadow.
  - Add-ons: primary-fixed, -fixed-dim, on-primary-fixed, on-primary-fixed-variant (same for secondary and tertiary); surface-dim; surface-bright. Background and surface-variant are legacy.
- **Usage rules:**
  - "On" roles are only for text and icons. "Container" roles are fills, never text.
  - Primary: highest emphasis (FAB, filled button). Secondary: lower emphasis (selected navigation indicator, tonal button). Tertiary: contrasting accents (badges, input emphasis).
  - Error is static by default.
  - Outline is for important boundaries such as text fields (≥3:1). Outline-variant is for decorative dividers, and for chip/card borders only when inner content provides 4.5:1.
  - Hyperlinks use primary (or tertiary) and are always underlined.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Tone-based surface-container roles replaced elevation tint in Feb 2023: +1 → low, +2 → container, +3 → high, +4/+5 → highest.
  - Defaults: low for elevated button/card; container for top/bottom bars; high for FAB and basic dialog; highest for text-field fill and switch-off track.
  - **Keep surface mappings the same for each layout region across breakpoints**: body = surface, navigation = surface-container.
  - Surface-dim and surface-bright keep their relative brightness across light and dark; for example, a nav rail on surface-dim beside a chat pane on surface-bright.
- **Schemes:**
  - **Static baseline** (purple 3P baseline, primary #6750A4 family) or **custom brand** via Material Theme Builder.
  - **Dynamic:** user-generated (wallpaper) or content-based (album art, "fidelity" tones).
  - Up to **two** colour-source types per screen. Never replace semantic colours with content colour.
  - Harmonization shifts static colours' hue toward primary within limits (red stays red).
- **Use static baseline "if your product is built for iOS"**, or if the product is enterprise, or is migrating from M2. [m3 choosing-a-scheme] This is directly relevant to the NEAR AI iOS target.
- **Expressive colour:** "vibrant" schemes and `MenuDefaults.itemVibrantColors()`. There is a stronger push to contrast primary, secondary and tertiary in one view (e.g. purple + green) for hierarchy.

### 3.2 Elevation [m3 elevation][cx]
- Levels:

  | Level | dp |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | 0 |
  | 1 | 1 |
  | 2 | 3 |
  | 3 | 6 |
  | 4 | 8 |
  | 5 | 12 |

  Resting elevation uses levels 0–3; levels 4–5 are only for interaction (hover, drag). Hover raises an element +1 level; for example, a FAB goes 3 → 4.
- Tokens carry no shadow or colour. "Each platform determines the specific shadows." Elevation can be shown by **tonal difference (the default in M3)**, by shadow, or by scrim.
- Use shadows only to protect elements on busy backgrounds, or to invite interaction (lift on drag).
- **Scrim:** the scrim role at **32%** opacity.
- Resting levels per component:

  | Level | Components |
  |---|---|
  | 3 | Date and time pickers, modal dialogs, FAB, extended FAB, FAB menu close button, search |
  | 2 | Scrolled app bar, menu, nav bar, rich tooltip, toolbar |
  | 1 | Banner, modal bottom sheet, elevated button/card/chip, modal drawer, modal side sheet |
  | 0 | Everything else, incl. app bar at rest, filled/tonal/outlined buttons, cards, carousel, lists, nav rail, docked side sheet, sliders, tabs |

- Android motion avoids scale and z-axis on enter/exit "since they imply elevation change, which doesn't match M3's reduced elevation model".

### 3.3 Icons: Material Symbols [m3 icons]
- A variable icon font in 3 styles: **outlined, rounded, sharp**. It has 4 axes:
  - **Weight** 100–700. Minimum 200 at 24dp; don't mix weights.
  - **Fill** 0–1. Use it for selected or unselected states, e.g. in navigation.
  - **Grade** −25 to +200. 0 for dark icons on light; **−25 for light icons on dark** to offset bleed; positive grade for active emphasis. Match grade to the text grade.
  - **Optical size** 20, 24, 40, 48dp. Use 20dp for dense desktop and 40–48dp for hero actions.
- **Grid:** 24dp with a 20dp live area and 2dp padding. Keylines: square 18dp, circle 20dp, vertical rectangle 20×16dp, horizontal rectangle 16×20dp. Corners 2dp; stroke 2dp (weight 400); sharp style uses 0dp corners.
- **With text:** use the same size and optical weight as the text, and **shift the symbol baseline down ~11.5% of the text size**.
- Navigation items always need labels. Icons below 20dp need labels unless they are simple (e.g. stars).

### 3.4 Motion [m3 motion/*][tok][cx][mdc]
**Motion physics system (current).** A spring has stiffness, damping ratio and initial velocity, with mass 1.
- There are 2 schemes, **expressive** (recommended default; overshoots) and **standard** (functional; minimal bounce). Each has 3 speeds (fast/default/slow) × 2 types:
  - **Spatial:** position, size, rotation, corner radius, shape. These may overshoot.
  - **Effects:** colour and opacity. No overshoot (damping 1.0).
- Tokens: `md.sys.motion.spring.{fast|default|slow}.{spatial|effects}` (+ `.damping` and `.stiffness`). The scheme is chosen at product level and is not part of the token name.

| Token | Expressive damping / stiffness | Standard damping / stiffness | Web cubic-bezier + duration (Expressive) | Web (Standard) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fast.spatial | **0.6 / 800** | 0.9 / 1400 | (0.42, 1.67, 0.21, 0.90) 350ms | (0.27, 1.06, 0.18, 1.00) 350ms |
| default.spatial | **0.8 / 380** | 0.9 / 700 | (0.38, 1.21, 0.22, 1.00) 500ms | (0.27, 1.06, 0.18, 1.00) 500ms |
| slow.spatial | **0.8 / 200** | 0.9 / 300 | (0.39, 1.29, 0.35, 0.98) 650ms | (0.27, 1.06, 0.18, 1.00) 750ms |
| fast.effects | 1.0 / 3800 | 1.0 / 3800 | (0.31, 0.94, 0.34, 1.00) 150ms | same |
| default.effects | 1.0 / 1600 | 1.0 / 1600 | (0.34, 0.80, 0.34, 1.00) 200ms | same |
| slow.effects | 1.0 / 800 | 1.0 / 800 | (0.34, 0.88, 0.34, 1.00) 300ms | same |

Sources: [tok] and [cx ExpressiveMotionTokens / StandardMotionTokens]. Both agree. The web curves are from [m3 motion specs]. MDC-Android Views ship only the **standard** values as `?attr/motionSpring*` [mdc].

- **Speed usage:**
  - Fast: small components such as switches and buttons, and the colour of a switch handle.
  - Default: partial-screen motion such as bottom sheets and expanded nav rails, and the opacity of rail content.
  - Slow: full-screen motion and full-screen refreshes.
  - "All component motion is driven by two tokens: expressive fast spatial and expressive fast effects." On Compose, **21 components** use the physics system by default.
- **Tokens are device-relative:** "the exact values of each token differ depending on if the device is a wearable, phone, or tablet." The physics parameters, not fixed times, make motion "feel fast in the context of the device".
- **Customization levels:**
  1. use expressive or standard;
  2. define a custom MotionScheme;
  3. override the scheme per element via CompositionLocal, e.g. standard inside a utilitarian screen.
- **Why springs:** they can be interrupted and retargeted, and carry velocity through, unlike tweens.
- **Apple spring conversion** [inf]: SwiftUI `Spring(duration:bounce:)` maps with stiffness = (2π/duration)² and damping ratio = 1 − bounce, for mass 1.

  | Scheme | Spring | Apple equivalent |
  |---|---|---|
  | Expressive | fast spatial | duration ≈ 0.222s, bounce 0.40 |
  | Expressive | default spatial | ≈ 0.322s, bounce 0.20 |
  | Expressive | slow spatial | ≈ 0.444s, bounce 0.20 |
  | Standard | fast spatial | 0.168s, bounce 0.10 |
  | Standard | default spatial | 0.237s, bounce 0.10 |
  | Standard | slow spatial | 0.363s, bounce 0.10 |
  | Both | effects | 0.102 / 0.157 / 0.222s, bounce 0 |

  For comparison [apple-k]: SwiftUI `.smooth` is bounce 0, `.snappy` ≈ 0.15 and `.bouncy` ≈ 0.3, all with a 0.5s default duration. M3 Expressive springs are therefore **faster and, at "fast spatial", bouncier** than Apple defaults.

**Legacy easing and duration (still used for transitions; "no longer maintained").**
- Curves:

  | Curve | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Emphasized | path `M0,0 C0.05,0 0.133333,0.06 0.166666,0.4 C0.208333,0.82 0.25,1 1,1`. CSS and iOS have no equivalent and fall back to Standard |
  | Emphasized decelerate | (0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1) |
  | Emphasized accelerate | (0.3, 0, 0.8, 0.15) |
  | Standard | (0.2, 0, 0, 1) |
  | Standard decelerate | (0, 0, 0, 1) |
  | Standard accelerate | (0.3, 0, 1, 1) |
  | Legacy | (0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) |
  | Linear | (0, 0, 1, 1) |

- Durations: short1–4 = 50/100/150/200ms; medium1–4 = 250/300/350/400; long1–4 = 450/500/550/600; extra-long1–4 = 700/800/900/1000.
- Defaults:

  | Movement | Emphasized | Standard |
  |---|---|---|
  | Begin and end on screen | 500ms | 300ms |
  | Enter | decelerate, 400ms | decelerate, 250ms |
  | Exit | accelerate, 200ms | accelerate, 200ms |

  Use Emphasized for "exit temporarily" (e.g. a drawer). Selection controls use 200ms Standard. A FAB→sheet transform uses 400ms Emphasized; a card→full screen uses 500ms. Ambient auto-advance uses 1000ms.

**Transition patterns.**
- **Container transform:** for hero moments and shallow hierarchies; cards, lists, FAB→sheet, search.
- **Forward/backward:** "Both Android and iOS should use platform defaults". Android slides with a fade; **iOS uses parallax**.
- **Lateral:** tabs, carousels; no fade.
- **Top level:** a quick fade-through for nav bar, rail and drawer. Never lateral, which conflicts with swipeable content.
- **Enter/exit:**
  - Android components expand and collapse along x or y, avoiding scale and z.
  - **iOS components "uniformly scale as they enter and fade out to exit"**, and slide off-screen without changing shape.
  - Components enter from their edge: menus expand downward from the top, snackbars upward from the bottom.
- **Skeleton loaders:** a pulse running top-left → bottom-right; content then fades in quickly.
- **Rules:** honour reduced motion (use fades; disable parallax and **shape morph**). Clean fades: fade out fully before fading in. Use one primary axis. "Common transitions should not use overt style effects like bouncy springs." Jump cuts only for pure-efficiency cases.

### 3.5 Shape [m3 shape][tok][cx]

**Corner radius scale (10 steps).**

| Step | Radius |
|---|---|
| none | 0 |
| extra-small | 4 |
| small | 8 |
| medium | 12 |
| large | 16 |
| **large-increased** | **20** |
| extra-large | 28 |
| **extra-large-increased** | **32** |
| **extra-extra-large** | **48** |
| full | circular |

The bolded steps are new in Expressive.
- Directional variants: extra-small-top, large-top/start/end, extra-large-top.
- Asymmetric "**inner corners**" are used for grouped items (menus, split buttons, connected button groups, segmented lists) and always map to individual corner tokens.
- **Optical roundness:** outer radius − padding = inner radius (example: 48 − 14 = 34). This is the same rule as Apple's concentricity; see §9.
- Customize by changing a style's value or remapping a component to another style. The shape family can be rounded or **cut**. Avoid large or full corners on information-dense containers such as cards.

**Shape library: 35 shapes** [cx MaterialShapes].

| Group | Shapes |
|---|---|
| Basic | Circle, Square, Slanted, Arch, Fan, Arrow, SemiCircle, Oval, Pill, Triangle, Diamond, ClamShell, Pentagon, Gem |
| Sunny | Sunny, VerySunny |
| Cookies | Cookie4Sided, Cookie6Sided, Cookie7Sided, Cookie9Sided, Cookie12Sided |
| Organic | Ghostish, Clover4Leaf, Clover8Leaf, Burst, SoftBurst, Boom, SoftBoom, Flower, Puffy, PuffyDiamond |
| Pixel | PixelCircle, PixelTriangle |
| Other | Bun, Heart |

- Use them for decorative crops, avatars, hero visuals and loading indicators; "avoid applying unconventional shapes to text-heavy containers".
- **Shape is "versatile, not semantic":** don't assign fixed meanings to shapes.
- **Shape morph:** any library shape can morph into any other. It uses the expressive scheme by default.
  - Built into the **standard button group** (press morph) and the **loading indicator**.
  - Compose added morphing filter and input chips in 2026.
  - Use it for interaction states, progress, and environmental change.
  - **Web: not available** (Compose `MaterialShapes` / graphics-shapes only).
- **Principles:** embrace tension (mix round and sharp); shape and type "share roundness" (Google Sans Flex ROND axis); shape can be 2.5D (layered motion).

### 3.6 Typography [m3 typography][tok][cx][dg]

**30 styles: 15 baseline + 15 emphasized**, across 5 roles (display, headline, title, body, label) × 3 sizes. The scale is Major Second (1.125) on a 14sp base. Values from androidx-main:

| Style | Size / line height (sp) | Baseline weight | Emphasized weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display L/M/S | 57/64, 45/52, 36/44 | 400 | 500 |
| Headline L/M/S | 32/40, 28/36, 24/32 | 400 | 500 |
| Title L | 22/28 | 400 | 500 |
| Title M/S | 16/24, 14/20 | 500 | **700** |
| Body L/M/S | 16/24, 14/20, 12/16 | 400 | 500 |
| Label L/M/S | 14/20, 12/16, 11/16 | 500 | **700** |

- Tracking (3P Roboto, sp):
  - Display L −0.25 (−0.2 in androidx-main);
  - Title M 0.15 (0.2 in androidx-main); Title S 0.1;
  - Body L 0.5; Body M 0.25 (0.2 in androidx-main); Body S 0.4;
  - Label L 0.1; Label M and S 0.5;
  - others 0.

  Emphasized styles use tracking 0 for display, headline and title-large. With 1P and variable fonts, tracking is 0 everywhere. [tok]
- **Variable type scale** (`md.sys.typescale.variable.*`): the font is **Google Sans Flex** (1P) or **Roboto Flex** (3P), with `wght` 400/500/600/700. Emphasized variable styles add **ROND=100** (rounded terminals) and step weight up by one: label and title-medium/small go 500 → 600 in variable, 500 → 700 in static. [tok]
- **Typefaces:**
  - brand (display, headline, title-large) vs plain (body, label, title-medium/small).
  - 3P default: Roboto for both. 1P: Google Sans + Google Sans Text.
  - **iOS context: plain = "System"** (i.e. SF Pro). [tok]
  - **Google Sans and Google Sans Flex were open-sourced in 2025.** Google Sans Flex has 6 axes: weight, width, optical size, slant, grade, roundedness. [dg]
- **iOS "Bold Text" accessibility:** every weight steps up 100 (400→500, 500→600, 700→800). [tok]
- **Font fallback:** Roboto Flex → Roboto → Noto Sans. Roboto Flex axes: slnt, wdth 25–150, wght, GRAD −200…+150, opsz, plus parametric XOPQ, YOPQ, XTRA, YTUC, YTLC, YTAS, YTDE and YTFI.
- **Language height (Aug 2026):** scripts fall into small (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew), medium (~7% taller; CJK, Arabic, Indic, Thai, Vietnamese), large (~30%; Burmese, Telugu) and extra-large (~100%; Nastaliq). Line-height tokens per category, e.g. body-large 24/27/31/41 and display-large 64/73/89/120. Default to **medium**. Fixed-height components are built for "small".
- **Emphasized usage:**
  - Use it for selection, actions, headlines, unread state, badges, primary-action buttons, extended FAB, and selected list and menu items.
  - Components don't use it by default; swap the token, e.g. `md.sys.typescale.emphasized.display-large`.
  - Don't go too heavy at small sizes.
- **Editorial treatments:**
  - "Standalone, showcase moments driven by type": celebrating content, the voice of the user, bespoke functionality (e.g. width and weight tracking a light dimmer).
  - Tokenise them for consistency; don't mix clashing styles; never use them for labels.
- **Units and readability:**
  - Web uses rem (sp/16); letter-spacing = tracking px / font size.
  - **Web and iOS typeset with bounding boxes and half-leading; Android uses baselines.**
  - Line height ≈1.2× for display, headline and title; ≈1.5× for body and label.
  - Use tabular figures for changing numbers.
  - Default text colour is on-surface (on-surface-variant as the alternative).

## 4. Cross-component system rules (apply to every component below)
- **States.**
  - State layer uses the content ("on") colour: hover 8%, focus 10%, pressed 10%, dragged 16%.
  - Disabled: container on-surface @ 10–12%; content @ 38%.
  - Focus ring: secondary, 3dp thick, 2dp outer offset (lists use −3dp, i.e. inside). [tok]
- **Targets.**
  - Minimum 48×48dp, enforced through layout padding even when the visual is smaller (XS and S buttons, 18dp chip icons, 6dp badges).
  - 44dp for pointer; 56dp for XR.
  - Selection controls use a 40dp state layer.
- **Elevation.** Tone first, shadow only when needed:
  - Flat components separate with surface-container steps.
  - Shadows remain on: FAB (6dp), dialogs and pickers (6dp), menus and rich tooltips and toolbars (3dp), modal sheets (1dp), snackbar (6dp).
- **Colour.** Selected and active states converge on **secondary-container / on-secondary-container**:
  - nav bar/rail indicator, chips, list selection, tonal buttons;
  - expressive menus and lists use **tertiary-container** for selection or reorder in their vibrant variants.
- **Motion.** Component motion = expressive **fast spatial** (0.6/800) for shape and size, plus **fast effects** (1.0/3800) for colour. Larger surfaces (sheets, expanded rail) use **default**. Transitions still use legacy easing.
- **Labels.** Sentence case; 1–3 words; no wrapping or truncation in buttons, nav or FABs. Navigation labels must remain readable at 2× text size.
- **Density.** Never by default; it is a user opt-in that keeps 48dp targets. 2026 Compose adds "precision pointer" sizing for desktop. [cx]

### 5.2 Actions

**Buttons** [m3][tok]
- **Use:** discrete actions. Style choice by emphasis:
  - filled: highest emphasis, one per page (Save, Confirm);
  - tonal: secondary;
  - elevated: only over busy backgrounds;
  - outlined: medium emphasis or "escape a flow";
  - text: lowest; dialogs and cards.
- **Anatomy:** container, label, optional leading icon (mirrors in RTL).
- **Sizes:**

  | Size | Height | Side padding | Icon | Icon–label gap | Type | Square radius | Pressed radius | Outline |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | XS | 32 | 12 | 20 | 4 | label-large | 12 | 8 | 1 |
  | **S (default)** | 40 | 16 (legacy 24 NR) | 20 | 8 | label-large | 12 | 8 | 1 |
  | M | 56 | 24 | 24 | 8 | title-medium | 16 | 12 | 1 |
  | L | 96 | 48 | 32 | 12 | headline-small | 28 | 16 | 2 |
  | XL | 136 | 64 | 40 | 16 | headline-large | 28 | 16 | 3 |

- **Shape:**
  - Round = full corners at every size. Square = the table's value.
  - **Press:** both shapes morph to the "pressed" radius using fast spatial 0.6/800.
  - **Toggle selected:** round becomes square, square becomes round.
- **Colour roles:**

  | Style | Default (container / content) | Toggle selected (container / content) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Filled | primary / on-primary | primary / on-primary |
  | Tonal | secondary-container / on-secondary-container | secondary / on-secondary |
  | Elevated | surface-container-low / primary (1dp) | primary / on-primary |
  | Outlined | outline-variant stroke / on-surface-variant | inverse-surface / inverse-on-surface |
  | Text | primary label | — |

  Filled toggle unselected = surface-container / on-surface-variant.
- **Elevation:** filled and tonal are 0, rising to 1dp on hover. Elevated is 1dp, rising to 3dp on hover.
- **Adaptive:** compact = end-aligned below content; large = start-aligned beside content. Avoid "long, flat" stretched buttons; cap the width.
- **A11y:** 48dp targets for XS and S; ≥3:1 contrast (container for filled styles, label for outlined and text); Space or Enter activates. Labels 1–3 words; never wrap.

**Button groups** [m3][tok]
- **Standard group:** related actions (Back / Pause / Next).
  - Gap XS 18, S 12, M/L/XL 8dp, chosen so 48dp targets hold.
  - **Pressed button widens +15%** (spring 0.6/800) and squeezes its neighbours.
- **Connected group:** selection, views, sort (replaces segmented).
  - Gap 2dp; outer corners full; inner corners XS–M 8dp (page says XS 4dp), L 16, XL 20.
  - Pressed inner corners: 4/4/4/12/16. Selected inner corners: fully round (50%).
  - Toggle buttons only; single-select, multi-select or selection-required.
  - Don't mix colour styles; XS and S get 48dp minimum width.
- Groups never wrap: overflow the trailing items into a menu. Container is invisible and not focusable; Arrow keys move within the group.
- Sizing modes: fixed (per breakpoint) or flexible (fill to max width). Connected spans its surface; add a max width on large windows.

**Split button** [m3][tok]
- Leading action plus a trailing menu chevron. Sizes XS–XL, styles elevated, filled, tonal or outlined.
- 2dp gap between halves. Inner corners at rest: 4/4/4/8/12; on hover or press: 8/12/12/20/20.
- Trailing width equals the height (48/48/56/96/136; XS and S padded to a 48dp target). Trailing icon 22/22/26/38/50.
- When open:
  - the trailing half's inner corners go fully round and the chevron centres and **rotates 180°**, using the *standard* motion scheme;
  - the menu sits 4dp away, aligned to the trailing button.
- A11y: the trailing half exposes an expanded/collapsed state and a label such as "More watch options".

  | XL | 32 / 48 / 72 | 104 / 136 / 184 |

- Square and pressed radii are the same as buttons.
- **Styles:** filled (default), tonal, outlined, standard (no container, on-surface-variant; selected = primary).
- **Toggle:** outlined icon becomes filled (or semibold) when selected, so two cues are used, not just colour.
- On web, show a tooltip with the action name on hover.

**Toolbars** [m3][tok]
- Height 64dp. Holds slots for icon buttons, buttons, text fields and images.
- **Docked:** full width, square corners, bottom only. Padding 16dp; item spacing 4–32dp (default 32).
- **Floating:** pill-shaped, 6dp elevation, 8dp internal padding, 4dp item spacing.
  - Margins ≥16dp horizontal or ≥24dp vertical.
  - Horizontal or **vertical** (vertical only for larger breakpoints, opposite the nav rail).
  - May pair with a FAB (8dp gap; FAB at 1–3dp elevation); never with an extended FAB or FAB menu.
- **Colour:**
  - Standard = surface-container, content on-surface-variant, selected secondary-container.
  - **Vibrant** = primary-container, content on-primary-container; use it for high emphasis or modes such as edit.
- **Scroll:** docked hides on scroll. Floating hides, or **collapses to a single key action or FAB** (Compose), but not both.
- **Rules:**
  - Never shown together with a navigation bar: the nav bar sits on primary pages, the toolbar on subsequent pages.
  - Emphasise one action only.
  - Overflow goes into a trailing menu.
  - Multiple toolbars only on large windows.
  - Web: `role="toolbar"`.
  - Android hide-on-scroll is disabled when TalkBack is on.

### 5.3 Navigation and top-level structure

**App bars** [m3][tok]
- Variants:

  | Variant | Height | Title type | Subtitle type |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Search app bar** | 64 (56dp pill search container) | — | — |
  | **Small** | 64 | title-large 22/28 | label-medium |
  | **Medium flexible** | 112, or 136 with subtitle | headline-medium 28/36, up to 2 lines | label-large |
  | **Large flexible** | 120, or 152 with subtitle | display-small 36/44 | title-medium |

- Icon 24dp, avatar 32dp, 4dp edge spacing, square container.
- **Actions:** one trailing action, two at most. More actions go to a toolbar; avoid overflow menus. Optionally one filled or tonal trailing icon button. Up to 4 trailing icons on large screens.
- **Search app bar:** the search container fills the space between leading and trailing elements up to 312dp, then grows to fill only 50% of it.
- **Scroll:** surface goes to **surface-container fill on scroll (no shadow)**. Flexible bars compress to small; the bar can hide or reveal, or go transparent-on-scroll with filled icon buttons.
- Colours: title on-surface, subtitle on-surface-variant, leading icon on-surface, trailing icons on-surface-variant.
- **Rules:** never round the container corners; full width always; never transform an app bar into a search app bar.

**Navigation bar (flexible)** [m3][tok][mdc]
- 3–5 destinations on compact (and medium two-pane); "not intended for desktop".
- **64dp tall** (was 80). Indicator 56×32dp, full radius, for vertical items. 24dp icon; 4dp icon–label gap; 6dp top and bottom padding. Label: label-medium 12/16.
- **Horizontal items** on medium windows: icon beside label inside a 40dp-tall pill, 16dp side padding. Items are content-width and centred; extra space goes to the ends.
- **Colours:**
  - container surface-container;
  - indicator secondary-container; active icon on-secondary-container;
  - **active label secondary** (changed in Expressive);
  - inactive icon and label on-surface-variant;
  - badges error.
- **Behaviour:**
  - Top-level fade-through transition. Re-selecting the active item scrolls to top. No swipe between destinations.
  - May hide on scroll, but not when a screen reader is active.
  - The icon fills when selected; the indicator expands from the icon centre.
- **Open question:** a selected label is "no longer bolded" per mdc, yet the a11y tab says "filled icon with a bold label". Pick one.
- Labels are mandatory and stay readable at 2×. Never combine with a rail or drawer.

**Navigation rail** [m3][tok][mdc]
- **Collapsed:**
  - width **96dp** (80dp narrow option);
  - items 64dp tall (56dp short) with a 56×32 indicator, 4dp between items;
  - 44dp from the top to the first element; ≥40dp from the header (menu or FAB) to the items;
  - label-medium labels.
- **Expanded:**
  - width **220–360dp**; horizontal items with a 56dp-tall indicator that hugs content, 16dp side padding, 8dp icon–label gap; label-large;
  - **the touch target spans the full rail width**.
- **Configurations:**
  - Expanded is either **standard** (pushes content; surface; 0dp) or **modal** (over a scrim; surface-container; 3dp; **16dp corners**). It can hide when collapsed.
  - Item groups are top- or centre-aligned; the menu button and FAB are always top-aligned.
- **Behaviour:**
  - The menu button toggles collapsed ↔ expanded, the FAB becomes an extended FAB, and adjacent content resizes.
  - Uses the default spatial spring (per the motion page).
  - Predictive back applies to the modal expanded rail only.
  - XR: becomes an **orbiter**.
- **Placement:** always the leading edge (right side in RTL); never horizontal. Badges sit on the icon when collapsed and beside the label when expanded.
- **A11y:** Tab or Arrow keys between items; label text visible up to 2×.

**Navigation drawer: NR.** "Use an expanded navigation rail, which has mostly the same functionality… and adapts better across breakpoints." The legacy spec, kept for reference:
- 360dp wide; 336×56 indicator with 28dp radius; 28dp padding; large-end 16dp corners;
- modal 1dp on surface-container-low; scrim neutral-variant20 @ 40%.

**Tabs** [m3][tok]
- Primary and secondary tabs, fixed or scrollable. Base, no Expressive changes.
- Height 48dp for label only; 64dp with icon + label (mdc says 72).
- Indicator: primary 3dp, secondary 2dp, top corners 3dp; primary inset 2dp with a minimum length of 24dp. Divider 1dp.
- Scrollable rows inset the first tab 52dp. Label: title-small.
- Colours: primary tab active = primary; secondary tab active = on-surface; inactive = on-surface-variant.
- Lateral transition. Avoid swipeable content inside. Arrow keys between tabs. Don't loop. Max 4 fixed tabs before switching to scrollable. On expanded and larger windows, tabs sit inside panes for secondary navigation.

**Search** [m3][tok]
- "Search bar + search view = **search**". Style **contained** (recommended; the bar persists inside focused search); "divided" is NR.
- **Bar:**
  - 56dp tall, pill-shaped, 360–720dp wide, surface-container-high, body-large text;
  - 30dp avatar on a 48dp target; at most 2 trailing elements.
  - **Margins 24dp, shrinking to 12dp when focused**, using fast spatial 0.6/800.
- **Focused layout:**
  - **Full-screen** on compact (surface-container-low).
  - **Docked** on medium and expanded: 360–720dp wide, 240dp up to 2/3 of screen height, results 2dp below in a 12dp-radius container.
- Results are lists, and gaps group them.
- Predictive back. Announce results. Hint text = accessible label ("Search your messages").

**Badges** [m3][tok]
- Small badge 6dp. Large badge 16dp tall, 16–34dp wide, max 4 characters ("999+"), label-small, 4dp horizontal padding.
- Colours error / on-error. Anchored to the icon's top-trailing corner:
  - offset 6×6 for the small badge;
  - offset 14×12 for the large badge.
- In the nav bar, hide the badge once the destination is viewed. Announced after the destination ("New notification" for dot badges).

### 5.4 Containment and surfaces

**Cards** [m3][tok]
- Variants: elevated (surface-container-low, 1dp), filled (surface-container-highest, 0dp), outlined (surface + 1dp outline-variant).
- **12dp radius**; 16dp padding; 8dp maximum between cards.
- Hover elevation 3dp elevated, 1dp otherwise. Dragged 8dp (elevated) or 6dp (others), per tokens; mdc differs.
- Content never scrolls inside a card.
- On compact, cards may swap to lists. Use container transform to open a card. A directly actionable card's role is button or link.

**Carousel** [m3][tok][cx]
- **Layouts:**

  | Layout | Items | Padding |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Multi-browse** | large + medium + small | 16dp side, 8dp top and bottom |
  | **Uncontained** | same-size items bleeding past the edge | 16dp leading only |
  | **Uncontained multi-aspect** (Nov 2025) | widths from 9:16 to 16:9 | 16dp leading only |
  | **Hero** | 1 large + 1 small | 16dp side |
  | **Centre-aligned hero** | 1 large + 2 small | 16dp side |
  | **Full-screen** | 1 edge-to-edge item | 0dp; 16dp gaps |

- Item gap 8dp. Small items 40–56dp. Item radius **28dp**. Large items have a configurable max width; a horizontal hero is at most 2× height wide.
- Compose 2026: `carouselParallaxScrollEffect`; `CarouselDefaults.ItemSpacing` and `ContentPadding`.
- **Adaptive:** more and larger items on larger windows; the full-screen layout is vertical on mobile.
- **A11y:** focus the first item, not the container. With reduced motion, add a "Show all" button (4dp padding) or a 48dp arrow next to the header. Never auto-advance without controls.

**Lists (expressive)** [m3][tok][cx]
- **Styles:** standard or **segmented**. Segmented items are filled tiles with **2dp gaps** instead of dividers.
- **Shape:**
  - 16dp outer corners and 4dp inner corners at rest;
  - **12dp on hover; 16dp when selected, pressed, focused or dragged** (shape morph).
- **Heights:** 1-line 56, 2-line 72, 3-line 88dp. Content is top-aligned at ≥88dp or 3 or more lines.
- **Padding:** 16 side, 10 top and bottom; 12dp between elements.
- **Element sizes:** expressive icons 20dp (baseline 24); avatar 40dp; image 56dp with an 8dp radius; video 100×56 or 114×64dp.
- **Colours:**
  - items on surface;
  - **selected = secondary-container / on-secondary-container**;
  - **reorder (dragged) = tertiary-container**;
  - focus ring drawn inside (−3dp).
- **Interactions:**
  - Single- or multi-action, single- or multi-select, non-interactive.
  - Expand/collapse.
  - Swipe-to-reveal (Android Views only; actions are 16dp-radius tiles).
  - Reorder: drag handle plus a non-drag alternative.
- Slots (leading, content, trailing) need custom code and are "not accessible by default".
- Keep line length 40–60 characters, up to 120 on large screens. On larger windows, lists may become list-detail, cards or a carousel.
- Web roles: listbox and option for selection.

**Dialogs** [m3][tok]
- **Basic dialog:**
  - **28dp** radius; 280–560dp wide; 24dp padding; 6dp elevation; surface-container-high;
  - headline-small title, body-medium text, 8dp between buttons;
  - custom position anywhere within a 56dp margin.
- **Full-screen dialog:**
  - compact only; max 560dp; 0dp radius;
  - 56dp header and action bar, which rise to 3dp on scroll.
  - Container: surface per tokens, surface-container-high per page.
- **Adaptive:** a full-screen dialog becomes a basic dialog at medium and above. In XR, use basic dialogs only.
- **A11y:** focus moves into the dialog, Tab wraps last → first, Esc closes, focus returns to the trigger. Basic dialog role on web = alertdialog.
- Scrim role at 32%.

**Bottom sheets** [m3][tok]
- **Standard:** no scrim; coexists with content; preset heights.
- **Modal:** surface-container-low, 1dp, scrim. Opens at ≤50% of screen height, then can be pulled to full.
- **Measurements:**
  - **Top corners 28dp**; max width **640dp**; 72dp top margin.
  - Once the window is >640dp, the sheet sits **56dp** from the top and sides.
  - **Drag handle 32×4dp** at on-surface-variant 40%, with 22dp above and below = a 48dp interactive band.
- **Behaviour:**
  - Tapping the handle cycles heights; tapping the scrim closes.
  - A non-drag alternative is required (a handle that acts as a button).
  - Predictive back.
- Compose 2026: unified `rememberBottomSheetState`; developers explicitly control the PartiallyExpanded anchor; new standalone static sheet.
- **Adaptive:** compact and medium use a bottom sheet. Expanded and desktop **swap to a side sheet**, or the docked pane becomes floating or co-planar. For supplemental selection, use a menu on medium and up.

**Side sheets** [m3][tok]
- **Standard:** docked, co-planar, 0dp radius, surface.
- **Modal:** surface-container-low, 1dp, **16dp inner corners**, scrim.
- **Detached:** 16dp inset, all corners 16dp.
- Max width **400dp** (token default 256dp). 24dp padding; 72dp bottom action area.
- At XL, a standard side sheet serves as the third pane. At compact, prefer modal.
- Role: dialog (modal); focus is trapped.

**Divider** [m3][tok]
- 1dp **outline-variant** (one token says surface-variant).
- Variants: full-width, inset, middle-inset; horizontal or vertical.
- Expressive guidance: **prefer gaps and containment**; reserve dividers for uncontained or complex lists and section boundaries.

**Chips** [m3][tok][cx]
- Types: assist, filter, input, suggestion; flat or elevated.
- **32dp tall, 8dp radius**, 18dp icons. Padding 16dp, or 8dp beside an icon; 8dp gap.
- Input chips: 24dp avatar. For two actions, use an 88dp minimum width so each gets a 48dp target.
- Outline: **outline-variant** (since Aug 2024). Selected filter and input chips: secondary-container.
- Elevated chips: surface-container-low at 1dp.
- 2026 Compose: shape-morphing filter and input chip overloads.
- Use a chip row or side sheet for many filters. Never swap a button for a chip.

### 5.5 Communication, feedback and progress

**Menus (expressive vertical menus)** [m3][tok][cx]
- **Container:** **16dp** radius (unfocused submenu 8dp; it morphs back to 16 when focused); 3dp elevation.
- **Items:** 44dp tall; first and last items get 12dp outer corners and 4dp inner corners; 16dp side padding, 8dp vertical; 12dp gaps; **20dp icons**; label-large.
- **Groups:** 2dp gaps (Android only; web uses dividers).
- **Colour:**

  | Variant | Container | Selected |
  |---|---|---|
  | Standard | surface-container-low | tertiary-container |
  | **Vibrant** | tertiary-container | tertiary / on-tertiary |

- **Selection** changes shape (12dp) and colour; add a checkmark too.
- **Baseline menu:** 4dp radius, 112–280dp wide, 48dp items, surface-container.
- **Submenus:** placed beside the parent, never over it.
  - Compose 2026 adds cascading submenus.
  - The page says submenus are unavailable on Compose, which is now stale.
- **Placement:** expands away from the screen edge.
- **A11y:**
  - Focus goes to the first item.
  - Up/Down move between items; Right/Left open and close submenus; typing a letter jumps to an item; Esc closes.
  - Disabled items stay focusable.
- **Breakpoints:** bottom sheet on compact, menu on medium and up.

**Tooltips** [m3][tok]
- **Plain:** 24dp tall, 8dp padding, 4dp radius; inverse-surface with inverse-on-surface; body-small. Sits 4dp from the target (8dp if the target has no boundary); above the target, or below in app bars.
- **Rich:** surface-container, 12dp radius, 3dp elevation. Padding 12/16/8dp. Subhead title-small, body body-medium, up to 2 text buttons.
- Both disappear **1.5s** after the pointer leaves. One tooltip at a time.
- Trigger: hover, long-press or focus. A persistent rich tooltip is opened by click or tap.

**Snackbar** [m3][tok]
- 48dp tall for 1 line; 2 lines = 64dp (page) or 68dp (tokens). 4dp radius; 6dp elevation.
- inverse-surface, with an inverse-primary action. body-medium text; label-large action.
- Bottom placement, above FABs and toolbars.
- **Auto-dismiss after 4–10s unless it has an action** (then it persists). One at a time.
- A11y: **polite live region, no focus steal**. Web needs inline feedback or a keyboard shortcut.
- Not in Material Web.

**Progress indicators** [m3][tok]
- Linear or circular; determinate or indeterminate; **flat or wavy**; configurable thickness.
- **Linear:**
  - flat 4dp, wavy 10dp tall (amplitude 3dp, wavelength 40dp determinate / 20dp indeterminate), thick 8dp (wavy 14dp);
  - 4dp gap between indicator and track; 4dp **stop dot** at the track end, needed for 3:1 end visibility;
  - inset ≥4dp.
- **Circular:** 40dp flat; **48dp wavy** (amplitude 1.6dp, wavelength 15dp); thick 52dp; scalable 24–240dp.
- Colours: primary on a secondary-container track. Inside buttons, use the label colour and drop the track.
- **Use for waits >5s.**

**Loading indicator** [m3][tok][cx]
- **Use for 200ms–5s indeterminate waits** (show nothing below 200ms). Never becomes determinate.
- 48dp container, 38dp shape, scalable 24–240dp. Default style: primary. **Contained** style: on-primary-container on a primary-container circle; required for pull-to-refresh.
- **Morph sequence** [cx LoadingIndicator.kt]: SoftBurst → Cookie9Sided → Pentagon → Pill → Sunny → Cookie4Sided → Oval.
  - Morph interval 650ms with a 0.6 / 200 spring; +90° rotation per morph.
  - Plus a continuous 360° global rotation every 4,666ms (linear).
  - Determinate mode morphs Circle → SoftBurst.

### 5.6 Selection and input

**Checkbox** [m3][tok]
- 18×18dp box, 2dp radius, 2dp outline, 40dp state layer, 48dp target.
- States: unselected, selected, indeterminate, error. Selected = primary / on-primary. Error = error.
- Pressed ripple is inverted: unselected presses use primary; selected presses use on-surface.

**Radio button** [m3][tok]
- 20dp icon, 40dp state layer, 48dp target. Selected = primary.
- Arrow keys move **and select**, wrapping around.

**Switch** [m3][tok]
- **Track 52×32dp.** Handle 16dp (unselected, no icon), 24dp (selected or with icon), **28dp pressed**; 16dp icons.
- Colours:

  | State | Track | Handle |
  |---|---|---|
  | Unselected | surface-container-highest, 2dp outline | outline |
  | Selected | primary | on-primary (primary-container on press) |

- Motion: the handle uses fast spatial; colour uses fast effects. Expressive and standard schemes are both shown on the motion page.

**Sliders** [m3][tok]
- Configurations: standard, centred, range; "stops" (formerly discrete); **vertical** orientation; **inset icon** (M/L/XL standard only).
- Sizes:

  | Size | Track | Handle height | Track corner |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | XS | 16 | 44 | 8 |
  | S | 24 | 44 | 8 |
  | M | 40 | 52 (page) / 44 (tokens) | 12 |
  | L | 56 | 68 | 16 |
  | XL | 96 | 108 | 28 |

- **Handle:** a 4dp-wide bar that narrows to 2dp on press or focus, with a 6dp gap to the track; inner track corners 2dp.
- Stop dots 4dp. Value bubble 44×48dp on inverse-surface, label-large.
- XL is for "hero moments". No vertical range sliders. A 3:1 track-end indicator is required.
- Keys: arrows; Home/End; Space + arrow for large steps.

**Text fields** [m3][tok]
- Filled or outlined. **56dp tall.**
- Filled: 4dp top radius, surface-container-highest, active indicator 1dp (2dp focused).
- Outlined: 4dp radius, outline 1dp (3dp focused in tokens; 2dp in mdc).
- Padding 16dp, or 12dp with icons; 24dp icons.
- Type: label body-large, shrinking to body-small when floated; supporting text body-small.
- Error state = error colour plus an error icon. Never rely on colour alone. No input masks while the user is typing.

**Date pickers** [m3][tok]
- Docked: 360×456dp, 16dp radius, 48dp cells.
- Modal: 360×524dp, 28dp radius, 40dp cells, headline-large date.
- Modal input: 328×512dp. Elevation 6dp; surface-container-high.
- Selected = primary. Today = 1dp primary ring.
- Docked on medium and up; modal or full-screen on compact. Always allow typed entry.

**Time pickers** [m3][tok][cx]
- Dial: 256dp clock, 48dp handle; time selector 96×80dp in display-large.
- Input: 96×72 fields, display-medium. Period selector uses tertiary-container when selected.
- Vertical or horizontal layout. Switch to input mode when height is short; don't densify.
- Compose 2026 expressive TimePicker adds a **scroll** variant (ScrollField).

**Rules for the hybrid** [inf]
1. Classify layout by the **actual window or container width in u**, using the M3 five classes, on every platform:
   - iOS: GeometryReader or containerRelativeFrame;
   - web: container queries where possible, not only viewport queries;
   - Android: WindowSizeClass with `supportLargeAndXLargeWidth = true`.
2. Use Apple's binary size class only to choose **idiom-specific components** (tab bar vs sidebar, sheet vs popover). Example: Apple calls an iPhone Pro Max in landscape *regular width but compact height*, while M3 calls it *expanded width, compact height*. Use the height class to keep a single pane and a minimised bar there.
3. **Margins.** M3 uses 16u at compact and 24u everywhere else. Apple's readable and layout margins are 16–20pt on iPhone and 20+ on iPad. Use 16 / 24 / 24 / 24 / 24 with a readable-width cap. Both systems agree on 40–60 characters per line (M3) and on readable content guides (Apple).
4. **visionOS** sits in Large. Use the M3 XR rules alongside the Apple ones:
   - comfortable field of view: M3 says centre 41°, panel 5° below eye level;
   - navigation moves out of the window: M3 orbiters ↔ visionOS ornaments;
   - targets ≥56u (M3 XR) vs 60pt eye targets (Apple) [apple-k]. Use 60.

### 8c. Conflicts between M3 Expressive and Liquid Glass, with recommended resolutions

**1. Surfaces: opaque tonal containers vs translucent glass.**
- M3 builds hierarchy from opaque **tone steps**: surface → surface-container-lowest…highest. "Surface tint" is deprecated, and colour roles keep the same mapping per layout region across breakpoints.
- Liquid Glass makes **controls and navigation a floating translucent layer**. It uses lensing, blur and specular highlights in regular and clear variants, and Apple says "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer" [HIG materials].
- *Resolution:* a **two-layer rule**.
  - **Content layer** (cards, lists, panes, sheets' inner content) uses M3 tonal surface roles on every platform.
  - **Functional/chrome layer** (top bar, bottom nav, rail/sidebar, toolbars, primary action, search field, menus/popovers, sheet chrome) uses **glass**:
    - native on Apple;
    - `backdrop-filter` blur plus a tinted semi-opaque surface-container on web;
    - RenderEffect blur on Android 12+.
  - Every glass token has an **opaque fallback that equals the M3 role**, e.g. glass-chrome → surface-container. The fallback is used for Reduce Transparency, low-end devices, unsupported browsers and high-contrast mode. This keeps trace-commons' glass identity while staying accessible and fast.

**2. Elevation and shadow.**
- M3 has 6 abstract levels (0/1/3/6/8/12dp), mostly rendered by tone. Shadows appear only where needed. Hover = +1 level; levels 4–5 are for interaction only.
- Liquid Glass separates layers by lensing and adaptive shadow. visionOS uses real depth.
- *Resolution:* keep **elevation as a semantic z-index token (0–5)**, rendered per platform:
  - glass with system shadow (Apple);
  - tone, plus a shadow only for FAB, menus and dialogs (Android/web);
  - spatial offset (XR/visionOS).
- Keep M3's "+1 on hover" and "no shadow on bars" rules.

**3. Shape: corner scale and shape tension vs concentricity and capsules.**
- M3 uses a fixed 10-step radius scale, deliberate **round vs square tension**, a 35-shape decorative library, and asymmetric inner corners.
- Apple derives radii from the container and device corners (**concentric**: `ConcentricRectangle`, `containerConcentric`) and defaults controls to **capsules**.
- The two agree on nesting: M3's "optical roundness" rule is *outer radius − padding = inner radius* [m3 shape], which is the concentricity rule.
- *Resolution:*
  1. Make concentricity the **law for nesting**. Inner radius is computed as `max(r_min, r_outer − inset)`, not chosen from the scale.
  2. Take outer radii for content containers from the M3 scale: 12 card, 16 sheet-detached, 28 dialog/sheet top, 32 XR/visionOS window and spatial panel.
  3. Edge-anchored chrome on iOS uses device-concentric radii. On Android/web, use the M3 values or the device corner radius where available.
  4. Default control shape = **capsule (M3 "full")**, which is also M3's default.
  5. Reserve "square" buttons and the 35 shapes for **content-layer hero moments**, avatars, image masks and loading. Never use them for glass chrome.

**4. Press and selection feedback: shape morph vs glass interaction.**
- M3 Expressive buttons morph their corner radius on press (e.g. S: full → 8dp) and invert shape on selection. Standard button groups widen the pressed item by 15%.
- Apple glass controls respond with a scale/"flex", specular highlight and a fluid merge (`glassEffectID` morphing between grouped glass shapes). The corner radius does not change.
- *Resolution:* make press feedback a **platform token**:
  - `feedback.press = morph` on Android and web, using M3 values;
  - `feedback.press = glassInteractive` on Apple.
- Selection is shown by **fill + tint + icon fill (two cues)** everywhere. The round↔square selection morph stays Android/web only.
- Group-merge morphs (Apple) and width morphs (M3) are both acceptable "expressive" behaviours for the same grouped-button spec.
- Reduced motion disables all morphs on every platform; M3 says so explicitly for shape morph.

**5. Motion: M3 springs vs Apple springs.**
- M3 Expressive spatial springs are fast and bouncy, e.g. fast spatial ζ = 0.6, k = 800, about 0.22s with bounce 0.4. Apple UI defaults are calmer: `.smooth` has bounce 0 and `.snappy` about 0.15, both 0.5s [apple-k]. Glass morphs are fluid but subtle.
- Both systems agree that **navigation transitions should be platform default and not bouncy**. M3: "Both Android and iOS should use platform defaults for forward and backward navigation"; "Common transitions should not use overt style effects like bouncy springs."
- *Resolution:*
  - Define motion tokens as **(response, dampingRatio)** pairs, and generate Compose specs, SwiftUI `Spring(duration:bounce:)` (k = (2π/response)², ζ = 1 − bounce) and web curves from M3's cubic-bezier table or CSS `linear()` spring approximations.
  - Adopt **M3 "standard"** (ζ 0.9) as the default on all platforms. It is closest to the Apple feel and to a glass aesthetic.
  - Use **"expressive"** only for content-layer hero moments and small control feedback on Android/web.
  - Keep effects springs non-overshooting (ζ = 1).
  - Keep M3's speed semantics (fast = small controls; default = partial-screen; slow = full-screen), because they translate cleanly.

**6. Colour: dynamic tonal schemes and vibrant fills vs neutral glass plus system colours.**
- M3 generates 26+ roles from a source colour (HCT), supports wallpaper-based dynamic colour and three contrast levels, and Expressive pushes **vibrant fills** (primary-container toolbars, tertiary-container menus).
- Apple uses semantic system colours, an app accent tint, and glass that adapts to what lies beneath. Tinted glass is reserved for prominent actions.
- M3 itself recommends **static baseline colour "if your product is built for iOS"** [m3 choosing-a-scheme].
- *Resolution:*
  - Use M3's HCT and Material Color Utilities **to generate the NEAR AI brand role scheme** (static, with light/dark and standard/medium/high contrast) as the single source of truth.
  - Map roles to Apple semantics:

    | M3 role | Apple semantic |
    |---|---|
    | primary | accent / tint |
    | on-surface | label |
    | on-surface-variant | secondaryLabel |
    | outline-variant | separator |
    | surface / surface-container-* | system backgrounds (content layer) |
    | error | systemRed-aligned error |

  - Dynamic (wallpaper) colour is **opt-in on Android only**, for personal surfaces.
  - Vibrant fills are allowed in the content layer and on Android/web toolbars. On glass, express emphasis with a **tinted glass** prominent action only.
  - Tie M3 medium/high contrast to Apple **Increase Contrast**, and Reduce Transparency to the glass fallback.

**7. Typography: Roboto / Google Sans Flex scale vs SF Dynamic Type.**
- M3 has 30 styles. Body is 14–16sp; emphasized styles step up the weight; the variable axes include ROND.
- iOS uses SF Pro with Dynamic Type (Body 17pt; Large Title 34pt) [apple-k].
- M3 tokens already include an **iOS context**: plain face = "System", and Bold Text adds +100 weight [tok].
- *Resolution:*
  - Parent semantic roles = M3's 5 roles × 3 sizes, each with an emphasized twin.
  - Apple renders through **Dynamic Type text styles**, e.g. body-large → .body, title-large → .title2, headline-medium → .title, display-small → .largeTitle. Android/web use M3 sizes.
  - Brand (trace-commons) typeface for display and headline only; system font for body and label on Apple. Roboto Flex or Google Sans Flex (now open source) is a valid web/Android face.
  - Keep M3's language-height line-height tokens on every platform.
  - Support 200% text scaling with spacing held at 1× (M3), which matches Dynamic Type behaviour.

**8. Navigation architecture and the primary action.**
- M3 uses an opaque nav bar or rail, an expanded rail in place of the drawer, a **FAB** for the primary action, and a docked or floating toolbar on secondary pages.
- Apple uses a floating glass tab bar that can minimise, a sidebar-adaptable tab view on iPad, toolbars for actions, and **no FAB**.
- *Resolution:* define abstract slots per scaffold region and render each natively.
  - **Bars:** top bar and bottom nav.
  - **Rails:** side nav, toolbars, and the primary action.
  - **Panes.**
  - The M3 scaffold of bars, rails and panes is a good platform-neutral skeleton, and it already maps rails to XR orbiters, which are visionOS ornaments.
  - The primary action becomes a FAB on Android and web-compact, and a prominent glass toolbar button on Apple. "Toolbar never with bottom nav" holds on both.

**9. Scroll behaviour of bars.**
- M3 bars fill with surface-container on scroll, flexible app bars compress, and nav bars can hide.
- Apple bars stay glass with a **scroll edge effect** (blur/fade of content beneath), large titles collapse, and the tab bar can minimise.
- *Resolution:* one token `bar.scrolledTreatment` = {edgeEffect (Apple, web-blur), tonalFill (Android, fallback)}. Large → small title collapse is common to both.

**10. Targets and density.**
- M3: 48×48dp touch, 44dp pointer, 56dp XR. Density is opt-in, and Compose 2026 adds precision-pointer sizing.
- Apple: 44×44pt (minimum 28×28) on iOS, smaller controls on macOS, 60pt on visionOS [HIG accessibility][apple-k].
- *Resolution:* platform-native minimums.
  - Touch: 48u on Android/web, 44pt on iOS.
  - Pointer/desktop: ≥32u (never below WCAG 2.2's 24 CSS px).
  - Spatial: 60u.
  - Ship desktop density as an explicit setting, not a breakpoint side-effect. Both systems agree density should not change automatically with window size.

**11. Feedback components with no Apple counterpart (snackbar, FAB menu, chips, wavy progress, loading-indicator morph).**
- *Resolution:* keep them in the parent spec for Android and web. On Apple, map them to native patterns from 8b.
- Where the trace-commons glass identity needs a custom component (e.g. a glass transient-status banner), follow M3's accessibility rules (polite announcements, timing, focus) and Apple's material rules (functional layer only; clear glass needs a 35% dimming layer over bright content [HIG materials]; M3 scrim is 32%). These are close enough to unify as **one 32–35% scrim/dim token**.

**12. Expressiveness budget.**
- M3 Expressive encourages hero moments, tension and vibrant colour, but limits them to "one or two" hero moments per product and warns against too many tactics.
- Liquid Glass emphasises deference to content and a calm functional layer.
- *Resolution:* chrome stays calm (glass, standard motion, capsules). **Expressiveness lives in the content layer**: emphasized type, size, containment, shape-library imagery and expressive springs, limited to one or two hero moments per product (e.g. the NEAR AI agent "thinking" state could use the loading-indicator morph language). This also matches Google's own research caveat that expressive design must not break conventions ("Context still matters").
